the idea

Drops sell out, so the app is built around urgency: a live countdown on every new drop, and as few taps as possible from "I want it" to "paid".

Forest green and kms yellow come straight from the clothes, on warm off-white with a faint kms pattern behind every screen.

01 — Welcome

Wear the city

A full-bleed street portrait sets the tone before anything else. One line, two buttons: get started or sign in.

02 — Home

Drop o'clock

The current drop sits on top with a live "ends in" timer, then quick category chips (tees, tanks, tracksuits) and the product grid. A floating tab bar keeps everything one thumb away.

03 — Product

Front & back

Front and back side by side, the sale price with the discount badge, and a size picker that sits right above "Buy now".

04 — Checkout

Bag it

Quantities change in place, the promo code and free-delivery badge sit together, and the total is impossible to miss before "Pay now".

the launch

In motion

For launch day I cut the screens into an 18-second film: the welcome screen, the drop countdown, a size pick and an "Order placed" moment, ending on the kms★ star.
