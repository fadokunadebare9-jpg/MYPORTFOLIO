Main bio

I'm a designer and motion designer who gets a little too excited about launch day. ✨ I care about the small details that make a brand feel alive.

I'm Fadokun Adebare, and I study information technology at the University of Ilorin. I design identities, interfaces and launch films for brands like Modestly, kms★ and Honey Scotch Cakes.

I also design for clothing brands, from embroidered caps to football kit concepts, and I use AI visualization to show a garment before it's ever sampled. And because I write HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, I can take a design all the way to a working page.
