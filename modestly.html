the idea

Every piece is either ready to wear or made to measure, so the flow has to handle both without making either one feel like a detour.

And because payment and delivery across Nigeria can feel risky, checkout shows every fee before you pay: delivery, payment processing, the total.

Two ways to buy

Off the rack or made for you

Ready-to-wear pieces show real stock per size ("1 left", sold out). Made-to-order pieces swap the size row for a measurements form, in inches or centimetres, that can be saved to your profile for next time.

Checkout

No surprises

Two clear steps: delivery, then review & pay. Delivery shows where it ships from and the fee up front; payment shows the processing fee as its own line, then Paystack (card, transfer, USSD or bank) or the Modestly wallet.

Launch

It's live

For launch I also made the "Our website is live" poster and promo films, so the brand looked the same everywhere: serif wordmark, gold accents, real products from real designers.
