MEXMED

A telehealth app concept built around one promise: care that comes to you. See a doctor, add the extras you need and chat with them after, all in one calm place.

iOS app UI / UX Healthcare
Role
App UI/UX, promo film
Screens
5 core screens
Platform
iOS (iPhone 15 Pro)
Palette
Lilac, chocolate, white

the idea

Health apps often feel clinical and stressful. MEXMED should feel like a calm, trustworthy friend: soft lilac, warm chocolate brown, lots of white space and friendly illustrations.

Every screen answers one question: Who am I seeing? When? What will it cost? How do I reach them?

01 — Home

Hello, Sarah

The next appointment comes first: who, when, and two clear buttons, Join call or Reschedule. Below it, quick access to find a doctor, pharmacy, tests and records, plus a gentle daily check-in.

02 — Doctor

Know who you're seeing

Dr. Elena Gomez's page shows what matters before you book: a 4.9 rating, "available today", the next slot, the $45 fee and the languages she speaks. One dark button: Book Consultation.

03 — Cart & chat

No surprises, then a friend

The cart treats a consultation like a clear receipt: optional follow-up reminders (+$5) and prescription delivery (+$10), each a simple tick, with the total in big, bold type. After booking, the doctor is one message away in a friendly chat.

the launch

Coming soon on iOS

I also made a short promo film for MEXMED: the logo draws itself, then one iPhone glides through all five screens with small animations (a tap ripple, the rating counting up to 4.9, chat bubbles arriving), to an original calm soundtrack.
